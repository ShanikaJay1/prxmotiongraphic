# PRX Vault: Launch Copy

All copy follows the PRX brief's wording rules:
- Cashback is **confirmed**. Never "automatic", "instant" or "earned".
- Cashback, not points or rewards: no badges, streaks or "you earned!".
- PokitPal is never named.
- The register is premium and confident, not hype.

The only figure used is **1,500+ places**, taken from your own UI. Add real numbers (users, cashback confirmed to date, merchant count) when you have them.
