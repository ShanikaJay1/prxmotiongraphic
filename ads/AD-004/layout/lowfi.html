Story 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, about 9s. Top funnel, Awareness, Trending video, Footage. Four beats with a hard cut on each. No offer, and no music baked in. Three variations follow, each one row of four screens.

Variation 1: Top-left caption

The clips run full bleed. Copy is locked top-left at the first headline line (240 + 96px), so the eye lands in the same place on every cut. The end card dims the last clip and the CTA sits under the headline.

Variation 2: Centre punch

One big stacked line dead centre per beat, so the clip frames it and the words hit with the cut. 頂点へ sits top-right. The end card hard-cuts to an ink ground with the wordmark large in the middle.

Variation 3: Manga panel

An ink ground with each clip set inside a heavy-bordered panel, like a page of the manga, with the copy in the band underneath. The end card is a staggered collage of three panels from earlier beats, with the CTA below.
